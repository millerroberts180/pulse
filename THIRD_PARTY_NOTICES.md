## Mechvibes community sound packs

The Mechanical and Ambient typing sounds' keyboard boards are recipes
measured from these Mechvibes community packs: for each kind of key, how loud
21 noise bands are over the first 140 ms of a keystroke. No audio from the
packs is included in Pulse or in this repository. Pulse plays fresh noise
shaped by the measured levels.

| Board | Measured from |
| --- | --- |
| Creamy | "Creamy thingy" by yozyrexx |
| Velvet | "WOB Rainy 75 PRO (FR4 + Kailh Cocoa)" by laika_raika300years_otomad |
| Citrus | "Orange deluxe" |
| Alloy | "amazin keychron q3 bluex" by bluecxmbo |
| Crisp | "apex pro tkl bluex" by bluecxmbo |

How the measurements are made: [scripts/boards](scripts/boards/README.md).
