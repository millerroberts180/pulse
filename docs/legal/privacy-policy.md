# Pulse privacy policy

Last updated [date]

Pulse is a Mac app made by [owner's full legal name or business] ("we"). This
policy says what Pulse reads on your Mac, what it keeps, and what reaches us.

## In short

- Pulse has no account and never goes online. Nothing it reads or keeps is
  sent to us or anyone else.
- It never keeps page text, URLs, form values, accessibility labels or what
  you type.
- It keeps only your settings, on your Mac.

## What Pulse reads, and doesn't keep

With the Accessibility permission you grant, Pulse reads the kind, state and
position of what macOS exposes about the app in front: "a button", "a heading
at this height", "the bottom of the page". It uses this in memory, as you
point and scroll, to choose a tap or a sound.

- **Control labels:** it checks them in memory to spot risky actions such as
  "Delete".
- **Find bars:** it reads a browser's find bar ("Result 2 of 7") only for its
  two numbers.
- **Typing:** it uses the kind of each key you press (a letter, Return,
  Delete) and which modifier keys are down, never the characters.
- **Other apps' audio:** it asks macOS whether another app is playing audio
  or using the microphone, never what is playing or being said.

None of this is stored, logged or sent anywhere.

## What Pulse keeps

Only your settings, in Pulse's preferences. They stay on your Mac.

## Links and support

The links in Settings › About open in your browser. What those sites collect
is up to them. Support is Pulse's issue tracker on GitHub: what you post there
is public, and GitHub's own terms and privacy statement apply to it. If you
write to us there, we keep your message and our reply on the tracker for
[how long].

## Children

Pulse isn't directed at children under [13 / the age that applies], and we
don't knowingly collect anything about them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be at [privacy policy URL] with
its date. Pulse itself sends nothing, so a change can only affect what
support collects.
